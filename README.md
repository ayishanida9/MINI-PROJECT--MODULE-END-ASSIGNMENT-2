## Power BI Dashboard

### Overview
Created an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee attrition and identify workforce trends.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age

### Visualizations
The dashboard includes eight visualizations analyzing attrition by:
- Gender
- Marital Status
- Job Role
- Overtime
- Department
- Age Group
- Education Field
- Years at Company

### Interactive Features
Added slicers for Department, Gender, and Job Role to dynamically filter the dashboard and explore attrition patterns.

### Skills Demonstrated
- Data visualization using Power BI
- DAX calculations
- KPI creation
- Interactive dashboard development
- Data analysis and reporting

## Key Findings

Based on the HR Analytics Dashboard, the following observations were identified:

- **Overall Attrition:** The dataset contains 1,470 employees, with 237 employees who left the company, representing an attrition rate of 16.12%.
- **Gender Analysis:** The dashboard shows higher attrition counts among male employees than female employees.
- **Marital Status Analysis:** Single employees have the highest attrition count among the three marital status categories.
- **Job Role Analysis:** Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives show relatively high attrition counts compared with several other job roles.
- **Overtime Analysis:** The dashboard compares employees who work overtime with those who do not, helping explore the relationship between overtime and attrition.
- **Department Analysis:** Research & Development contributes the highest attrition count among the three departments, followed by Sales and Human Resources.
- **Age Group Analysis:** Employees aged 25–34 have the highest attrition count among the displayed age groups.
- **Education Field Analysis:** Life Sciences has the highest attrition count among the displayed education fields.

## Conclusion

The dashboard provides an interactive overview of employee attrition across demographic and professional categories. The findings highlight workforce groups that HR teams may investigate further to understand attrition patterns and develop appropriate employee retention strategies.
